Forge Initiate's Reference
Orb Reference Card
Rarity
COMMON
An unenchanted item with no rarity stamp. The default state of any fresh item before orbs are applied.
ENCHANTED
An item that has had orbs applied. Carries a rarity stamp — Rare, Epic, or Specialised — and may carry additional stamps depending on which orbs have been used.
CORRUPTED items cannot be targeted by any orb except Orb of the Forge.
Progression
Orb of Subtle Power
Adds up to 3 random enchants, upgrading the item from Common.
RequiresCommon item
ForgesRARE
Orb of Humming Power
Adds further enchants on top of a Rare item, pushing it to the next tier.
RequiresRARE
ForgesEPIC
Reroll & Modify
Orb of Chaos
Randomly rerolls the level of every enchant. Transcended enchants are unaffected.
RequiresEnchanted item
ForgesRerolls enchant levels — retains TRANSCENDED if present
Orb of Entropy
Strips all enchants and the rarity tag, resetting the item to Common. Transcended enchants and the TRANSCENDED stamp survive — but you cannot transcend again after wiping.
RequiresEnchanted item
ForgesResets to Common — retains TRANSCENDED if present
Orb of Blessing
Rerolls all non-transcended enchants and adds 3–5 fresh ones (60% · 3, 30% · 4, 10% · 5). Transcended enchants survive. Resets the one-use ADDED limit so Orb of Addition can be used again.
RequiresEPIC or SPECIALISED
ForgesClears ADDED stamp — retains TRANSCENDED if present
Orb of Addition
Adds exactly one random enchant to an item with an open slot. One use per item — using Orb of Blessing resets the limit.
RequiresEPIC or SPECIALISED · Open enchant slot · No ADDED stamp
ForgesADDED
Removal
Orb of Regret
Removes one random non-transcended enchant. Regret and Illusive Regret can never be used on this item again.
RequiresEnchanted item · No GRAVEN stamp
ForgesGRAVEN — retains TRANSCENDED if present
Orb of Illusive Regret
Choose which non-transcended enchant to remove. Right-click to open the selector.
RequiresEnchanted item · No GRAVEN stamp
ForgesGRAVEN — retains TRANSCENDED if present
Orb of Absolution
Removes one random non-transcended enchant with no GRAVEN penalty. If the last enchant is removed, the item resets to Common.
RequiresEnchanted item
ForgesNothing — retains TRANSCENDED if present
Utility
Orb of Repair
Fully restores the durability of any item or tool.
RequiresAny durable item with damage
ForgesNothing — durability only
Orb of Onymorph
Right-click to enter a custom name, then drag onto any item to apply it. Single use.
RequiresAny item
ForgesCustom name — no rarity stamp
Endgame
Orb of Transcendence
One random enchant becomes permanently immune — cannot be removed, rerolled, or wiped by any orb. 1 in 1,000 chance to transcend two enchants.
RequiresEPIC or SPECIALISED · 4+ enchants · One use per item
ForgesTRANSCENDED
Orb of the Forge
Creates an exact copy of the item. The copy cannot be re-forged. The original is untouched and can be forged repeatedly. Works on corrupted items.
RequiresEPIC or SPECIALISED
ForgesCOPIED on the duplicate
Orb of Corruption
Seals the item permanently — no orb can touch it again except Forge. Works on Common items. Outcome is random:

RequiresAny item
